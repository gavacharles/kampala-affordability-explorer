# Who can afford the housing on offer in Greater Kampala?

An interactive map of housing affordability in the Greater Kampala Metropolitan Area, Uganda.

**Open the explorer:** https://gavacharles.github.io/kampala-affordability-explorer/
**Play the tour:** https://gavacharles.github.io/kampala-affordability-explorer/#tour

Each parish is shaded by the share of its households who could not afford the typical home listed online. You can switch between renting and buying, between a share-of-income test and a basic-needs test, and change the share of income and the mortgage terms.

Short videos for sharing are in [`videos/`](videos/):
- `affordability_tour.mp4`: a 48-second tour of Greater Kampala, its districts and sub-counties
- `priced_out_threshold.mp4`: the share of households priced out as the income threshold rises from 10% to 80%
- `hundred_households.mp4`: what the typical listed rent would mean for 100 households

## About the data

The typical home is the median asking rent of 1–2 bedroom units, or the median asking price of houses and apartments, among 10,643 deduplicated online listings (Jiji, Real Estate Database, Uganda Property Centre; 2025–26). Parish incomes are modelled from the Uganda Bureau of Statistics' National Household Survey 2019/20 and the 2024 Census, uprated to 2026 prices. Asking prices describe the formal, titled market, not informal rentals.

From the PhD research *Priced out of the formal market: A housing affordability index for Greater Kampala, Uganda, from online listings and spatial machine learning* (University of Johannesburg, 2026).
